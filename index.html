PRIVACY POLICY

GOOGLE PLAY STORE COMPLIANT
🔒
Zero Personal Data Collected No names, emails, phone numbers, or passwords are requested or tracked.
💾 1. Local Device Storage

Your match scores, winning streak records, sound preferences, and custom symbol skins are saved solely on your local device via standard browser/app storage (localStorage). This data is never sent to any external servers.

🌐 2. Online Peer-to-Peer Matches

Online 2-Player games establish a direct, encrypted WebRTC peer connection (PeerJS) between both devices. No chat logs, match outcomes, or user identities are stored or monitored on any centralized server.

📢 3. Advertisements (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to show non-intrusive advertisements. Google AdMob may use standard anonymous device advertising identifiers in compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies and COPPA family guidelines.

📱 4. Device Permissions

• Vibrate: Provides haptic tap feedback during moves.
• Internet: Required exclusively for online multiplayer & AdMob ad delivery.
No camera, microphone, contact, or location permissions are ever used.
